# WatchRead

WatchRead is a companion book connected to a lecture recording, with source references and chapter checks.

## Language

**Companion book**:
A readable work organized into chapters and connected to the lecture it accompanies.
_Avoid_: Transcript dump

**Cue**:
A timestamped segment of a transcript containing the words associated with that interval.
_Avoid_: Chapter

**Citation**:
A reference from book content to a passage in its source recording or transcript.
_Avoid_: Truth certificate

**Chapter check**:
A learning question associated with a chapter and its supporting source references.
_Avoid_: Exam certification

**Restudy clip**:
A source interval offered for review after a chapter check.
_Avoid_: Generated explanation

**Claim**:
A single statement in the companion whose relationship to the lecture can be inspected separately.
_Avoid_: Verified fact

**Source passage**:
The exact words of one or more identified transcript cues, with timing only when timing exists in the source.
_Avoid_: Model-generated quotation

**Source support**:
The extent to which a source passage supports a claim; it does not establish whether the lecturer is factually correct.
_Avoid_: Truth score, proof of correctness

**Misconception**:
A specific misunderstanding represented by a wrong answer and addressed by a source passage.
_Avoid_: Learner diagnosis

**Recovery check**:
A different question about the same concept, offered after reviewing a restudy clip.
_Avoid_: Mastery certification

**Recovery trail**:
The record of an initial answer, the source revisited, and a subsequent answer about the same concept.
_Avoid_: Proven learning gain

**Needs review**:
Content whose source relationship is missing, ambiguous, contradicted, or no longer current after an edit.
_Avoid_: Low-confidence fact

**Prepared sample**:
A saved companion paired with an actual playable recording and matching transcript, available without generating it again.
_Avoid_: Live generation, fake lecture

**Source revision**:
A particular version of the lecture transcript against which citations were made.
_Avoid_: Latest source
